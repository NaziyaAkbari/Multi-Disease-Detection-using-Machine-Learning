How It Works

1

Register Your Account

Create your free Multi Disease Detection account by filling in your personal details — name, age, gender, state, and a profile picture. Takes less than 2 minutes. Your data is stored securely and never shared.

2

Login to Your Account

After registration, log in with your username and password. You will be redirected to your personal home dashboard where all 5 disease modules are available for screening.

3

Home Page — Choose a Disease

From the home dashboard, choose any of the 5 disease modules — Tuberculosis, Skin Disease, Malaria, Asthma, or Chickenpox. Each module has its own dedicated screening form.

4

Enter Symptoms or Upload Image

For symptom-based diseases (TB, Malaria, Asthma), answer simple Yes/No questions about your symptoms. For image-based diseases (Skin Disease, Chickenpox), upload a clear photo of the affected area. Also select your Indian state for hospital suggestions.

5

AI Model Processes Your Data

Our trained Machine Learning models analyze your inputs. Symptom-based diseases use Random Forest classifiers. Image-based diseases use MobileNetV2 deep learning CNN models trained on medical image datasets from Kaggle.

6

View Your Result

Your result page shows the prediction (Positive/Negative or detected condition name), confidence score, suggested medicines with dosage info, health tips, and nearby hospitals in your state that specialize in that disease.

7

View Prediction History

All your past predictions are saved in your history page. You can track all screenings with disease name, prediction result, confidence score, date, and uploaded images — all in one place.

8

Contact Us Anytime

Have questions or feedback? Use the Contact Us page to send a message. Our team at Kalaburagi, Karnataka will respond within 1-2 business days at naziyaakbari7@gmail.com or call +91 7019050568.
