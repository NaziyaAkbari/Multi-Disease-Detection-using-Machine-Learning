About Multi Disease Detection

Intelligent Disease Detection Powered by Machine Learning

Our Mission

Multi Disease Detection AI was built with a single goal — to make early disease detection accessible to everyone in India. By combining the power of Machine Learning with a simple, easy-to-use interface, we help people screen for common diseases from the comfort of their home. We believe early awareness saves lives, and technology can bridge the gap between patients and healthcare.

What We Do

Tuberculosis

Symptom-based screening using Random Forest ML model trained on clinical datasets.

Skin Disease

Image-based detection using MobileNetV2 deep learning model — detects Acne, Eczema, Melanoma, Psoriasis and more.

Malaria

Symptom analysis using predictive algorithms to identify malaria risk quickly.

Asthma

Respiratory symptom evaluation to assess asthma likelihood and provide actionable advice.

Chickenpox

Both image-based and symptom-based detection using CNN and ML models respectively.

Hospital Locator

State-wise nearby hospital suggestions across all 28 Indian states for every detected condition.
